Common questions

Can you move our data from QuickBooks or Sage?+
  • Yes. We map your chart of accounts, customers, vendors and opening balances, then reconcile totals before go-live.
How long does an Odoo project take?+
  • It depends on the scope. We agree on a plan with milestones at the start, so you know the timeline before work begins.
What do I need to bring to my appointment for partnership, sole proprietor or personal taxes?+
  • All your T slips for the year: T3, T4, T4A, T5, charitable donations, RRSP, T2200 and any other relevant information.
  • Sole proprietor: your annual revenue and expenses, plus vehicle and home office expenses if applicable (see T2125).
  • Rental property: your annual revenue and expenses (see T776).
  • If you are not sure whether a document is relevant, bring it anyway.
